The growth in services exports exceeding imports is mostly seen in nominal terms. However, the annualised percentage growth shows that service exports only exceeded goods exports in 2022

CC4. Replicating. Re-create, then improve someone else's chart

I chose this chart of exit poll undertaken in 2020 displayed by RTE. The chart is misleading as the beige bars on the left appear to be showing the percentage share indicated but it is actually the bars on the right.Thus even though brexit, for instance, appears to be the most important factor, it is actually the least, at 1%.

I have tried to replicate the chart using vega-lite. it is a normalized 100% stacked bar chart with the colors red and beige as shown in the image (json code was generated with the assistance of chatGPT). I was unable to flip the bars to be on the same side as in the image, and to place the percentages on the red bars so I left them at the bottom of the chart.

I have improved the chart by simply using a bar graph and ordering the arrangement from the largest to the smallest number. This makes the graph easier to read and interpret (json code was generated with the assistance of chatGPT).
